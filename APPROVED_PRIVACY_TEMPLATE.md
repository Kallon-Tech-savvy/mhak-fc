# MHAK FC privacy approval checklist — DRAFT ONLY

Before publishing, a responsible operator must supply `approved-privacy.html` at the project root. The release builder will **not** accept the current `public/privacy.html` draft as an approved notice.

The approved HTML should accurately describe:
- the identity of the responsible club operator, verified official email and response/contact route;
- purposes and categories of information collected (adult-only initial enquiries);
- providers/processors used (Cloudflare/Turnstile, selected email service, inbox provider);
- access controls, retention period, deletion process and how to request corrections/deletion;
- handling of children's enquiries (via responsible adult, no sensitive data);
- effective date and approval record.

Save the approved content as `approved-privacy.html` only after human review; do not put sensitive operational details or secrets into it. The script checks for obvious missing sections but cannot determine legal compliance.
