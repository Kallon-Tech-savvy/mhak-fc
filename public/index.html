01 / OUR BEGINNING

A club begins long before the first whistle.

We're laying the foundations for MHAK FC: a football club intended to offer young people a shared identity, standards they can trust and an environment in which to improve.

Our vision is ambitious. Our present reality is simpler. We're developing the club's structure, exploring coaching arrangements and defining the principles that will guide training and participation.
