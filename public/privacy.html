DRAFT NOTICE — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLIC ENQUIRIES

MHAK FC / SITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice (draft)

Current status: This page is a preparatory draft, not an approved privacy policy. The online enquiry form remains disabled and does not collect information while staging.

Planned contact

The planned website is mhak.alhajikallon.com and the intended club inbox is mhak@alhajikallon.com. These details require operational verification before launch.

Information the planned adult enquiry form would collect

Enquiry reason, the sender’s name, reply email address, their message, and confirmation that they are an adult who accepts this notice. The form is not a player-trial application and must not be used to submit a child’s personal details, government IDs, medical histories, dates of birth or full addresses.

Purpose

If activated, MHAK intends to use that information only to read, route and respond to relevant initial enquiries. A bot-protection provider would also process security tokens.

Processing and recipients

Cloudflare would provide site hosting and Turnstile verification. A configured transactional email provider would transmit enquiries to the authorised MHAK mailbox. The mailbox provider may retain messages. No website database storage is planned at launch.

Retention and requests (not yet approved)

Before activation, the club must approve a specific mailbox retention period, deletion process, authorised staff access, request-handling procedure, and a named privacy/safeguarding contact. These operational details are not yet confirmed, so this draft is not a basis for collecting visitor information.

Enquiries involving minors

The proposed form is restricted to adults. A parent or guardian can make an initial enquiry without sharing identifiable or sensitive information about a child. Any future youth programme will require its own safeguarding and consent processes.
