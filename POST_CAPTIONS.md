# MHAK FC — First three founding stories (drafts)

These are proposed narratives. Check that each factual statement still reflects MHAK FC's real stage before publication.

## Chapter 01 — Every club has a beginning

Every football club has a beginning. This is ours.

MHAK FC is an emerging football club in Sierra Leone. We are beginning with a belief: that shared identity, discipline and collective responsibility should shape how a club is built.

There is much still to do — finding the right people, establishing a practical training structure and earning trust through action. We intend to document that process, including the lessons and challenges.

Follow MHAK FC as we build from the ground up.

**Suggested visual:** `templates/01_Founding_Story.svg`
**Discussion prompt:** What should a new football club do first to earn players' trust?

## Chapter 02 — Different individuals. One identity.

Players arrive with different ambitions, experiences and strengths. But a team needs more than individual talent.

At MHAK FC, we want belonging to mean shared standards and responsibility — including for the people who lead the club.

This remains a principle we are working to put into practice, not an achievement we are claiming in advance.

**Suggested visual:** `templates/02_Belonging.svg`
**Discussion prompt:** What makes you feel part of a team?

## Chapter 03 — No shortcuts

It's easier to announce ambition than to build the systems behind it.

Our work starts with the basics: identifying suitable coaching support, defining our standards, and organising a realistic development environment.

We will share genuine milestones as they happen. Progress is more meaningful when it can be seen and verified.

**Suggested visual:** `templates/03_Standards.svg`
**Discussion prompt:** What matters most to you in a coach or training environment?

## Editorial guardrails

- Do not imply MHAK already has a confirmed coach, a registered squad, an academy or regular training when those things remain unconfirmed.
- Use only authentic photos/footage with permission; avoid using photographs of unrelated people as if they were MHAK players.
- Do not publish minors' names, faces or contact details without appropriate safeguards and permission.
- Keep social handles, registration links, dates and venue details out of drafts until they are verified.
